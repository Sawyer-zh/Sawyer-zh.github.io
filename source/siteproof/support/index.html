SiteProof Support

SiteProof helps contractors and field professionals turn job-site photos and notes into clear, client-ready PDF reports.

Contact support

Email sawyerzhang2017@gmail.com with your SiteProof version, device and system version, steps to reproduce the issue, and a screenshot if it contains no confidential customer information.

Where is my project data stored?

Projects, notes, and photos are stored locally on your device. SiteProof does not require an account or upload project content to a SiteProof server.

How do I share a PDF report?

Open a project, build the PDF report, and tap Share report. Use the iOS or iPadOS share sheet to save it to Files, send it through an installed app, or use AirDrop.

How do I restore SiteProof Pro?

Open Settings in SiteProof and tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the Apple Account that originally purchased SiteProof Pro.

How do I change advertising privacy choices?

Open Settings → Ad privacy options. Available controls depend on your region and Google's consent requirements. SiteProof Pro does not display ads.

Can SiteProof recover a deleted project?

SiteProof does not keep a server copy of your projects. A deleted project may not be recoverable. Keep copies of important reports and maintain an appropriate device backup.
