Task Hours Support

Getting started

Create a project, tap Start Work, pause during breaks, then Finish Session. Add missed sessions from the iPhone detail screen. Pro adds hourly-rate estimates and CSV export. Estimates are not invoices or payroll calculations.

Paired-device sync

Open both apps on your paired devices and tap Sync Now. Offline changes are saved locally. When the same item is changed on both devices, the latest revision determines its current state; history from both devices is retained.

Pro and restore

Open Unlock Pro on your iPhone and choose Restore Purchases with the Apple Account that owns your purchase. Verified access syncs to the paired Watch. Existing records remain accessible if Pro is revoked.
