Pocket Draw Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 12, 2026

Pocket Draw does not require an account. The free iPhone app includes Google AdMob advertising and Google User Messaging Platform for consent. Apple Watch does not display ads. We do not collect your lists or draw results on a developer-operated server.

Lists and results

Your list names, options and latest draw rounds are saved on your device. The app transfers your list library and Pro access status between your paired iPhone and Apple Watch using Apple's WatchConnectivity framework. Each device maintains its own draw round. Device backups may include local app data according to your Apple device settings.

Purchases

Apple processes payments through the App Store. Pocket Draw uses StoreKit to verify Pro access on your device. We do not receive your payment card information and do not operate a purchase-validation server.

Permissions and tracking

Pocket Draw does not request location, contacts, microphone or HealthKit access and does not request permission to access the advertising identifier (IDFA).

Advertising

Google's advertising SDK may process IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device identifiers, advertising and product interactions, and diagnostic information such as crashes and performance. This supports ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. The app requests non-personalized ads; this does not mean that no data is processed. List names, options and draw results are not passed to the advertising SDK by Pocket Draw.

Where required, consent options are presented before ads can load. Ad Privacy Options appears in Help & Privacy when available. Pro removes in-app advertising. Google explains its processing at Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

You can delete a list on iPhone and sync the change to Apple Watch, or clear the current round using Start Over. Removing the app removes its local app data; an unconnected Watch may retain its copy until synchronized or the Watch app is removed.

Support

If you choose to email support, we receive your email address and whatever information you include. This is used to respond to your request. Do not include sensitive information in support messages.
