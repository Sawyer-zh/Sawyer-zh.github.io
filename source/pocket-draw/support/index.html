Create a list

On iPhone, tap New List, add a name, and enter one option per line. Options must be unique. Tap Save and open the list to Pick One.

Use Apple Watch

Install Pocket Draw on your paired Apple Watch, open the iPhone app, and tap Sync Lists. Transfers may wait until the devices can communicate. Once a list arrives, you can draw offline. Rounds are separate on each device.

Heads and Tails

Heads and Tails are the two sides of a coin. Each draw has an equal chance of either result.

Start Over

Start Over clears the current result and returns every option to the pool. It does not delete your list.

Pro and restore

Pro is a one-time App Store purchase that unlocks multiple lists, no-repeat draws, random ordering, and removes iPhone ads. Open Unlock Pro on iPhone and choose Restore Purchases using the Apple Account that made the purchase. Sync Lists to update Watch access.

Price unavailable

Check your connection and tap Retry App Store. No charge occurs just by retrying. Contact support if the problem continues.

Reporting a problem

Include your app version, iPhone or Apple Watch model, system version, and the steps that led to the problem. You can attach a screenshot, but remove private list names or other personal information before sending it.
