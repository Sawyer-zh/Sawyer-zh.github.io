Row Count

A simple iPhone and Apple Watch tool for keeping your place in everyday life.

Record with one tap, undo a mistake, and keep records on your paired devices. The free version supports 3 active items. Pro is a one-time purchase for unlimited projects, extra counters, repeating patterns and CSV export, and removes iPhone ads. Apple Watch has no ads.

Requires iOS 17 or later and watchOS 10 or later.
