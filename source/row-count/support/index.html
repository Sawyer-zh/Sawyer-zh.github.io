Row Count Support

Getting started

Create an item, open it, and tap the main action to record. Undo cancels the latest active record. Archive completed items to keep your list tidy.

Apple Watch sync

Install the companion app on your paired Watch. Open both apps once, then tap Sync Now. Changes are kept locally while devices are disconnected and merge when connected. If sync is unavailable, keep both apps open and try again.

Pro and purchases

Purchase or restore Pro on your iPhone using the Apple Account that owns the purchase. Open Unlock Pro, then choose Restore Purchases. Pro access syncs to the paired Watch. Pro includes unlimited projects, extra counters, repeating patterns and CSV export, and no iPhone ads. There is no subscription. Existing records remain available if Pro is revoked.

Export

With Pro active, use Export CSV from the iPhone main list. Choose a destination in the system share sheet.

Privacy options

Ad Privacy Options appears in the iPhone main list where required. Records are not sent to our servers.
