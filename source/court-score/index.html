People and teams

With Pro, save people for singles and teams for doubles or volleyball. Team results stay with the team and do not change individual win rates.

See your results

Pro profiles show wins, losses, win rates, recent form, and match history. Saved people can review head-to-head results.

More ways to follow the match

Score from iPhone or Apple Watch.

Prepare a match on iPhone and send its setup to a paired Apple Watch, or start on the device you will use to keep score. Updates sync when the paired devices can communicate.

Court Score Pro also adds English score announcements, an external scoreboard for a compatible display, export, and removal of iPhone home-screen ads. Pro is an optional one-time purchase.
