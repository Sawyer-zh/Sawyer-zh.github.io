Court Score Privacy Policy

Your match records stay on your devices. Court Score does not require an account.

Effective October 15, 2026

About this app

The free iPhone app includes Google AdMob advertising and Google User Messaging Platform for consent. Apple Watch has no ads.

Matches and saved records

Names you enter, saved people and teams, rules, scores, and match history are stored locally. Court Score uses Apple WatchConnectivity to transfer match setup and data between your paired devices. We do not operate a server that receives these records. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple settings.

Purchases

Apple handles the optional Court Score Pro purchase. Court Score verifies access with StoreKit on your device. We do not receive payment card details or operate a purchase-validation server. Pro unlocks saved people and teams, statistics, score announcements, an external scoreboard, export, and removal of iPhone home-screen ads.

Advertising

Google advertising SDKs may process IP addresses and approximate location, device identifiers, ad and product interactions, crashes, and performance information to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. Court Score requests non-personalized ads; this does not mean no data is processed. Match names and scores are not supplied to the advertising SDK by Court Score. Consent options are presented where required before ads load. Ad Privacy Options is available in Help when required. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Exports

When you choose Export Match or Export History, you control where the record is shared using the system share sheet. Review names and results before sharing.

Permissions

The app does not request location, contacts, microphone, or HealthKit access. It does not start a workout for the person keeping score.

Removing data

Removing the app removes its local data. Remove it from both devices to remove both local copies. Existing device backups are managed through your Apple settings.
