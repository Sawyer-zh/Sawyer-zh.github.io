Court Score Privacy Policy

Effective September 12, 2026

Court Score does not require an account. The free iPhone app includes Google AdMob advertising and Google User Messaging Platform for consent. Apple Watch has no ads.

Matches and names

Team names, rules, scores and match history are stored locally. The app uses Apple WatchConnectivity to transfer match data and setup between your paired devices. We do not operate a server that receives these records. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple settings.

Purchases

Apple handles Pro payments. Court Score verifies access with StoreKit on your device. We do not receive payment card details or operate a purchase-validation server. Pro removes iPhone ads and enables match record export.

Advertising

Google advertising SDKs may process IP addresses and approximate location, device identifiers, ad and product interactions, crashes and performance information to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. Court Score requests non-personalized ads; this does not mean no data is processed. Match names and scores are not supplied to the advertising SDK by Court Score. Consent options are presented where required before ads load. Ad Privacy Options is available in Help when required. See Google Privacy Policy.

Exports

When you choose Export Match or Export Match History, you control where the record is shared using the system share sheet. Review names and results before sharing.

Permissions

The app does not request location, contacts, microphone or HealthKit access. It does not start a workout for the person keeping score.

Removing data

Removing the app removes its local data. Remove it from both devices to remove both local copies. Existing device backups are managed through your Apple settings.

Support

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send, solely to respond to your request. Contact sawyerzhang2017@gmail.com.
