Court Score Support

Start a match

Choose New Match on the device you will use to keep score. Choose the sport, team names, number of games and first serving team.

Prepare your Watch

On iPhone, choose Prepare Apple Watch Match, edit the setup and send it. On Watch, open New Match, check the settings, and tap Start Match. Transfers may wait until the paired devices can communicate.

Score and undo

Tap a team to add one point. Undo reverses the last action, including switching ends or starting the next game. Match results are saved locally after every change.

General scoring

Choose General to set a target from 1 to 100 and optionally require a two-point lead. Choose best of 1, 3 or 5 games. This mode has no serving side or automatic change-ends prompt. Undo works as in the sport presets.

Rules

Badminton uses 21 points, win by two, capped at 30. Table tennis uses 11 points, win by two. For doubles, service indicates a team; individual player positions are not tracked.

Sync and history

The recording device owns the match. The other device is read-only. Tap Sync to retry. The latest 30 matches recorded on each device are sent to its paired device; older matches remain on the recording device.

Pro and Restore

All scoring, undo and custom General rules remain free. Court Score Pro is a one-time purchase that removes iPhone ads and unlocks individual match and history export. Open Unlock Pro on iPhone to buy or Restore Purchases with the Apple Account used for purchase. If the price is unavailable, check the connection and retry; no purchase occurs just by retrying.
