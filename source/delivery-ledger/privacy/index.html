Privacy policy

Effective September 11, 2026 · Operator: Hang Zhang

Delivery information

Customer names, addresses, quantities, notes, photos and signatures are stored locally on your device. Delivery Ledger does not operate a server that receives these records. No account is required for the delivery workflow. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple backup settings.

Permissions

Camera access lets you take delivery photos. The system photo picker lets you select images to copy into a receipt. The app does not request location access or upload your photo library.

Sharing and backups

When you share a PDF or export a backup, the information goes to the app or destination you choose. Those services have their own privacy practices. Backups contain customer details, photos and signatures; choose a destination you trust.

Purchases

Apple processes purchases. The app checks purchase transactions to enable Pro and restore access. We do not receive your payment card details.

Advertising

The free plan uses Google AdMob when advertising is enabled and available. Google's SDK may process IP addresses and approximate location, device identifiers, ad interactions, product interactions, crash data and performance diagnostics for advertising, analytics and service operation. Delivery record text, photos and signatures are not sent to AdMob by this app.

The app does not request access to the advertising identifier through an App Tracking Transparency prompt. Where required, advertising consent choices are presented before eligible ad requests. You can revisit available choices through Advertising privacy choices in Settings. Pro removes in-app ads.

Read Google's Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

Retention and deletion

Records remain on your device until removed with the app. Drafts can be deleted in the app; finalized receipts are retained with separate revisions for corrections. Uninstalling deletes local app records. Copies you exported or shared and device backups must be managed at their destinations. Preserve a backup before uninstalling if you need your history.

Support

If you email support, we receive the information you send and use it to respond and troubleshoot. Do not include unnecessary customer information. For questions or requests concerning support messages, contact sawyerzhang2017@gmail.com.

Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted here with an updated effective date.
