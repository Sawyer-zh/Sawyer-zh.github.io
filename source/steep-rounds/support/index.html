Steep Rounds Support

Getting started

Create a tea recipe with its first infusion duration and the seconds to add per infusion. Start an infusion, then tap Finish & Next Infusion when ready. Reset Tea Session starts again from infusion one while retaining history.

Timer alerts

Tap Enable Timer Alerts to permit local notifications on the device you use. Your notification settings, Focus modes and device state can affect delivery. Timers store their deadline and remain accurate when the app reopens. Parking reminders also include a five-minute warning when started with more than five minutes remaining.

Paired-device sync

Open both apps on your paired devices and tap Sync Now. Offline changes are saved locally. When the same item is changed on both devices, the latest revision determines its current state; history from both devices is retained.

Pro and restore

Open Unlock Pro on your iPhone and choose Restore Purchases with the Apple Account that owns your purchase. Verified access syncs to the paired Watch. Existing records remain accessible if Pro is revoked.
