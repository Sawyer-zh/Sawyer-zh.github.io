Silent Stage Privacy Policy

Effective September 12, 2026. Contact: sawyerzhang2017@gmail.com.

Templates and history

Silent Stage stores templates and timer history locally and transfers them between your paired iPhone and Apple Watch using Apple Watch Connectivity. We do not operate a server that receives this history. The app retains up to 20 sessions and up to 500 events per session. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple settings.

Diagnostics and support

Technical events include session times, stage names, app state, and haptic requests. They cannot confirm physical vibration delivery. Logs remain on your devices unless you choose to export and share them. If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and attachments to handle your request. Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to necessary legal retention.

Advertising and consent

The iPhone app integrates Google AdMob and User Messaging Platform. Google's advertising SDK may process IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP, device or app identifiers, advertising interactions, crash information, and performance data for advertising and related analytics. We request non-personalized ads; this does not mean no data is processed. Consent choices are requested where applicable, with Ad Privacy Options available when required. Pro removes in-app ads. Timer history and HealthKit data are not supplied by our app to advertising services.

See Google's privacy policy and Google's SDK data disclosure.

Purchases

Apple processes purchases. The app checks verified transaction information to determine Pro access. We do not receive your payment card information.

Health permissions

Exercise mode requests permission to use workout sessions for real exercise. Silent Stage does not save workouts to Health and does not request access to read your health history. Talk sessions use extended runtime and do not require workout authorization. Health data is not used for advertising.

Your choices

You can delete templates, control permissions in system settings, and remove locally stored app data by deleting the app from both devices. Previously exported copies and device backups are managed separately. No Silent Stage account is required.

Website

This website has no advertising or analytics scripts. Hosting infrastructure may process standard request information such as IP addresses for serving and securing the website.

Updates

We will update this page when our practices change.
