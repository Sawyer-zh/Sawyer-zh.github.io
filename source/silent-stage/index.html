Silent Stage

Stage timers for iPhone and Apple Watch.

Create preparation countdowns, custom stages, and repeated rounds. Follow wrist cues while keeping your attention on your presentation or exercise.

Keep your timing in view

Talk sessions support wrist-down cues for up to one hour, including pauses. Keep Silent Stage open on your Watch: leaving the app interrupts the session. Cues are supplementary, not guaranteed in every system state.

Free basic timers. Pro adds unlimited custom templates and removes iPhone ads with a one-time purchase. Availability and local pricing appear in the app.

Requires iOS 17 and watchOS 10 or later. Currently in testing.
