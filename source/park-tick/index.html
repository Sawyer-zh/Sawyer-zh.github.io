Park Tick

A focused tool for iPhone and Apple Watch.

Create a parking preset and add your floor or space number. Start Parking begins the reminder; Extend 15 Minutes changes the reminder only. This app does not pay for or extend paid parking and does not guarantee compliance with parking rules.

Free to start

Keep three active projects or presets and use the complete core workflow. No account is required. Apple Watch works locally and syncs with your paired iPhone.

One-time Pro

Unlock unlimited projects or presets, CSV history export, and remove iPhone ads. No subscription. Purchase or restore on iPhone.
