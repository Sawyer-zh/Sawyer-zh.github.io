Last Done

A simple iPhone and Apple Watch tool for keeping your place in everyday life.

Record with one tap, undo a mistake, and keep records on your paired devices. The free version supports 5 active items. Pro is a one-time purchase for unlimited activities, categories and CSV export, and removes iPhone ads. Apple Watch has no ads.

Requires iOS 17 or later and watchOS 10 or later.
